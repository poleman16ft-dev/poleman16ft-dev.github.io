Help test KL Travel

KL Travel is a trip planner for Android. Before it can go public on Google Play, it needs 12 people to test it for 14 days. Thank you for helping!

  1. Send me your Gmail address. It must be the Google account your Android phone uses for the Play Store. Email poleman16ft@gmail.com and I'll add you to the test list.
  2. Join the test. Once you're added, open this link while signed in with that account and tap Become a tester:
    Join the test
  3. Install the app from Google Play:
    Get KL Travel
  4. Keep it installed for 14 days and open it now and then. That's all Google needs.
  5. Optional: tell me what's confusing or broken. Feedback goes to poleman16ft@gmail.com.

Testing is free. The app has no account and no server: your trips stay on your phone. Privacy policy · Home
