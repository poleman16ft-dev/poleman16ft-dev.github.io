KL Travel · Android

Privacy policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · package com.kl.travel · developer KL Ventures

The short version

  • KL Travel has no server and no user accounts of its own. Your trips, settings, email passwords and documents stay on your phone.
  • The app talks directly to the services you set up (your Google Sheet, your email, Google Maps and the others listed below). Only the data each feature needs is sent, and only to that service.
  • The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Ads are the only third party that receives data you did not choose to send.
  • There is no analytics or tracking SDK. Crash reports stay on your phone unless you choose to share one.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your trips, events, lodging, expenses and settings.
  • API keys you enter (Google Maps, flight status, Claude), kept in the app's private storage.
  • Email account details for the email import, with passwords encrypted on the device.
  • Receipt photos and expenses. Receipt text is read on your phone with Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition; the photos are never uploaded.
  • Documents and photos you add to the Vault, behind your screen lock.
  • A crash report, if the app crashes (see below).

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Android cloud backup of the app's data is turned off. The in-app Backup feature saves a file only where you choose (for example your own Google Drive or Files). Email passwords and Google sign-in are never included in a backup.

These services handle your data under their own privacy policies. The developer never receives, sells or shares your data.

Ads

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information, such as the advertising ID, IP address and app activity, to show and measure ads. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in More › Settings › About › Privacy choices (ads). Buying Pro removes ads completely, and no ad requests are made after that. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Location

Location is used only when you pick "My location" as the starting point on an event, to work out routes and leave-by times from where you are. It is sent only to Google's routing service for that purpose and is not stored.

Permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate): optional, only for "My location" as a starting point.
  • Notifications: optional, for leave-now and flight alerts the app creates on your phone.
  • Internet and network state: to reach the services above.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a description of the error (the app version, the phone model and Android version, and the technical error) is saved on your phone. It is never sent automatically. You can share it from More › Settings › About, using the app you choose, or delete it there.

Children

KL Travel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Each feature is off until you set it up, and you can remove any key, email account or sheet in Settings.
  • You can change ad consent in Settings, or remove ads with Pro.
  • You can delete everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.
